# Six production recipes

1. Chinese beginner:6-seat preflop with explicit blinds, one complete main line through settlement. Explain in action-free beats before decisions. Generate Chinese voice with default IndexTTS, or intentionally attach external audio; render landscape and export project.
2. English heads-up:button/small blind, correct pre/postflop actor. Attach audio once, render both aspects. Compare terminal RenderPlan states; layout does not change the hand.
3. Japanese unequal all-ins:complete river snapshot, distinct cards and unequal stacks. Explain public information before showdown, then pots/returned excess after reveal. Independently account for money in evidence; attach audio, render portrait.
4. Short all-in:separate scenes/routes for insufficient versus cumulative reopening. Seek original bettor, inspect legalActions. Record an intentionally rejected raise as a negative test, then deliver legal lines.
5. Branch/translation:branch from neutral decision, unique IDs and own voice. Revise one language, confirm only its audio invalidates, regenerate/attach it and render selected branch. Check other languages separately.
6. Handoff/recovery:export, reopen at different path, check saved output. Remove a copied asset, verify detection and reattach. Retry an exact committed request and compare receipt/revision. Cancel render with SIGTERM; retry after cleanup and verify one published result.

Keep authored JSON, source narration, NDJSON requests/responses and final receipts in task evidence. Completion requires actual files, not a successful schema check alone.
